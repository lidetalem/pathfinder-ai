Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Pathfinder (“we”, “us”, “the site”) is a web app that helps people explore content creation and freelancing paths through a short questionnaire. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it simple because we collect very little.

1. Information we collect

  • Google sign-in information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account identifier. We only ask for basic sign-in information. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.
  • Your answers and results. The pathway you choose, your answers to the questions, your progress and your result summary, so you can continue later.
  • Contact messages. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and/or phone number, and the message you type.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and security tools process technical details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and prevent abuse (for example, limiting how many messages one person can send).
  • Browser storage. We store a sign-in session token and your light/dark theme choice in your browser so the site remembers you. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and show your name.
  • To save your progress and show your results.
  • To read and reply to messages you send us.
  • To keep the site secure, stable and free of abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Google user data

Pathfinder’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google profile information only to provide the sign-in and personalized features described above.

4. Service providers

We use trusted companies to run the site. They process data only to provide their service:

  • Google – sign-in.
  • Neon – database hosting.
  • Render – server hosting.
  • Vercel – website hosting.
  • Resend – delivering email notifications of contact messages to us.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account details and results while you use Pathfinder. We keep contact messages for as long as needed to respond and keep a reasonable record. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see section 6).

6. Your choices

  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us (see section 9).
  • You can restart your assessment inside the app at any time.
  • You can review or remove the apps connected to your Google account in your Google Account settings.
  • You can clear your browser storage to remove the session token and theme choice from your device.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to our database and rate limiting to protect your information. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Pathfinder is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Contact us

Questions or requests about your data? Use the contact form on the Pathfinder site, or message us on Telegram (@lidetalem) or LinkedIn.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above when we do.
